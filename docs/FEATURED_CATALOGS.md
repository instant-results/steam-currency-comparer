## Selection evidence

- [Devolver's official overview](https://investors.devolverdigital.com/about-us/)
  describes its large indie/AA publishing catalog. Its [official about page](https://www.devolverdigital.com/about)
  explains its work with independent developers worldwide. Its [annual report](https://investors.devolverdigital.com/files/downloads-and-publications/Devolver-Digital-Annual-Report-2021.pdf)
  lists its Austin, Texas address. Existing examples are from Massive Monster,
  Askiisoft, and Dodge Roll.
- [THQ Nordic's official overview](https://thqnordic.com/company/about) identifies
  its Vienna base and broad publishing portfolio. Steam credits Bugbear for
  [Wreckfest](https://store.steampowered.com/app/228380/), Mimimi Games for
  [Desperados III](https://store.steampowered.com/app/610370/), and Haemimont Games
  for [Jagged Alliance 3](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1084160/).
- [11 bit studios' investor overview](https://ir.11bitstudios.com/en/who-we-are/)
  identifies Warsaw and its third-party publishing business. Examples deliberately
  use external studios: Digital Sun's [Moonlighter](https://store.steampowered.com/app/606150/),
  Dead Mage's [Children of Morta](https://store.steampowered.com/app/330020/), and
  Starward Industries' [The Invincible](https://store.steampowered.com/app/731040/).
- [Techland's official careers site](https://techland.net/company/careers) lists
  its Polish offices. Steam credits Techland as developer for
  [Dying Light](https://store.steampowered.com/app/239140/),
  [Dying Light 2](https://store.steampowered.com/app/534380/), and
  [Call of Juarez: Gunslinger](https://store.steampowered.com/app/204450/).

Anonymous Steam appdetails probes verified names, role credits, and non-free flags
for all nine newly selected games. Product-search probes verified nonempty catalogs
for developer=Techland, publisher=THQ Nordic, and publisher=11 bit studios in PL.
Icons were read from the organizations' official Steam pages. Load more uses the
exact credit name rather than a homepage alias (THQ Nordic Official and Techland
Publishing are homepage labels, not the matching credits for these role filters).

11 bit studios develops its own games too, but its external publishing examples
make it appropriate here. The selection criterion excludes almost exclusively
self-publishing studios, rather than requiring publishers never to develop games.
