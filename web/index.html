METHODOLOGY & DETAILS

How regional price comparisons
are calculated.

“Cheaper or equal” indicates that the baseline price is less than or equal to every selected foreign price converted to the baseline currency. This is a currency-conversion calculation, not an all-inclusive checkout quote.

What this comparison includes +
  • NBP table A mid exchange rates, with their publication dates. Non-PLN conversions divide the two PLN rates; their publication dates can differ.
  • No VAT or sales-tax adjustment. US and EU displayed prices may have different tax treatment.
  • No payment-provider fees or conversion costs. These are not checkout quotes.
  • Germany is our EUR storefront proxy, not a price for the entire euro area.
  • We check each storefront’s actual sale price. Regional offers are not assumed to share a discount; sale details arrive with the price, without an extra request.
  • Regional prices may be cached or fetched at different times. Sale badges and base prices are recorded independently for each storefront. Older cache entries may lack sale details; this does not mean the game is not on sale.

Difference compares the baseline storefront with the cheapest selected alternative. Negative means cheaper in the baseline storefront. The percentage uses that alternative’s converted price as its reference; it is unavailable when that price is zero. The “Different regional discounts” badge and filter identify unequal known discount percentages, including sale versus no sale. Missing sale details alone do not trigger the badge; checks made at different times can also explain a mismatch. Missing or failed regional prices are listed as uncompared.
